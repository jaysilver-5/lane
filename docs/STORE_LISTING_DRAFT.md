**Suggested title:** FirstLane: G1 Practice

**Suggested short description:** Independent Ontario G1 practice with explanations and study progress.

FirstLane helps you study Ontario road rules and signs through original practice questions and explanations. Try 10 fixed samples as a guest. An email-verified free account includes 40 fixed sample questions, explanations and saved practice progress.

Ontario G1 Complete is a separate one-time in-app purchase. Its Canadian reference price is CA$14.99; your store shows the final local price and applicable tax before purchase. It includes topic practice, learning rehearsals, mistake review, saved questions and downloaded study content. There is no automatic renewal or scheduled expiry on valid, non-refunded access. Future jurisdictions are separate purchases.

For offline study, download the Ontario pack while connected and reconnect at least every 30 days to refresh access. An internet connection is also needed for account actions, purchases, updates and optional progress sync. Questions about signs use text descriptions; study the official handbook's sign images too.

FirstLane is independent study software. It does not represent a government agency and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Ontario's Ministry of Transportation or DriveTest. FirstLane does not issue licences, submit government applications, book tests or appointments, or collect government fees. Practice questions and scores are learning aids, not official exam questions or guaranteed results.

Official reference material:

- Ontario driver's handbook: https://www.ontario.ca/document/official-mto-drivers-handbook
- DriveTest knowledge-test information: https://drivetest.ca/tests/knowledge-tests/

Intended for learners aged 16 or older. Do not use FirstLane while driving.
