# FirstLane advertising readiness — 14 October 2026

FirstLane has not been advertised or rejected by Google Ads. A prior restriction on another website has no established relevance to this product. The domain is newly purchased and the app is not yet publicly released.

## Policy position

FirstLane is independent Ontario G1 study software. It does not issue licences, submit applications, book government tests or appointments, or collect government fees. That supports an educational interpretation of the government documents and services policy, but Google has not confirmed this app's classification. The driving-training exclusion does not explicitly name standalone test-preparation apps. Do not claim that FirstLane is a government-authorized provider or driving school.

If the account or ads are classified under that policy, use the appropriate exclusion/out-of-scope application and complete the required advertiser verification. Do not change accounts or conceal the product to bypass enforcement. No Google account restriction is established by this review.

## Changes implemented

- Independent-study disclosures near download and purchase actions; no government affiliation or guaranteed result.
- Correct default account-deletion route: https://getfirstlane.com/delete-account.

## Launch gates

Both repository release checks must pass with production settings and evidence-based approvals. These checks include internal release standards; a failed check is not itself a Google policy verdict. Install ads require a recognized app available in the advertised stores/regions. Website acquisition ads require a functioning destination and available, accurately described offer.

No trackers are added by this work. If conversion measurement or remarketing is introduced, update the privacy/cookie disclosures and required consent handling before enabling it. Do not put personal data in advertising URLs or ordinary tracking tags.

A wildcard robots exclusion or noindex setting alone does not establish an AdsBot block: AdsBot ignores the global wildcard group. Test actual AdsBot access, redirects, WAF responses and HTTP status codes.
